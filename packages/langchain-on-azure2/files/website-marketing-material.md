# Welcome to API Playground

Welcome to **API Playground**, the friendly and fun platform where developers of all ages can explore, create, and integrate hosted APIs with ease. Our mission is to make API development as enjoyable as playing in a sandbox, providing you with the tools and resources to bring your ideas to life.

## Who We Are

At API Playground, we believe that building applications should be accessible and enjoyable for everyone, from budding coders to seasoned developers. Inspired by platforms like Firebase and Netlify, we've crafted a space where you can seamlessly integrate RESTful APIs and utilize our JavaScript SDK to enhance your projects.

## Our Offerings

We provide a suite of services designed to cater to your development needs:

- **RESTful APIs:** Access a wide range of APIs that allow you to manage data, authenticate users, send notifications, and more.
- **JavaScript SDK:** Simplify your development process with our easy-to-use SDK, enabling smooth integration of our services into your applications.
- **Real-Time Features:** Implement real-time functionalities like live chat and collaborative tools, ensuring instant data updates across all clients.
- **File Storage:** Store and manage media files, user uploads, and other data securely.
- **Cloud Functions:** Run serverless functions to extend your app's capabilities without managing infrastructure.
- **Usage Analytics:** Gain insights into user behavior to make informed decisions about your application's development.
- **UI Design Tools:** Design and customize your application's user interface with our intuitive tools.

## Service Tiers and Rate Limiting

To ensure fair usage and optimal performance, we offer various service tiers with corresponding API rate limits:

- **Free Tier:** Ideal for hobby projects and learning purposes. Includes basic access to our APIs with a rate limit of **100 requests per hour**.
- **Pro Tier:** Suitable for small to medium-sized applications. Offers enhanced features with a rate limit of **1,000 requests per hour**.
- **Enterprise Tier:** Designed for large-scale applications requiring high availability and performance. Provides premium features with customizable rate limits.

These tiers ensure that all users have access to the resources they need while maintaining the integrity and performance of our services.
