# Key Questions & Insights from User Data

Based on the user data analysis, here are three key questions that should be presented to management along with corresponding insights and growth opportunities:

---

## Question 1: Which features are most popular among our users?

**Insight:**  
- A preliminary review of the features_list suggests that **"ai"**, **"configuration"**, and **"cache"** appear frequently, indicating high user engagement with these functionalities.  
- **Growth Opportunity:**  
  - Emphasize development and refinement of these core features.
  - Consider bundling popular feature sets in higher-tier plans or creating add-on packages.
  - Use targeted marketing campaigns to showcase enhanced AI capabilities and configuration tools.

---

## Question 2: How engaged are users with our platform in terms of usage (queries per month) and the level of service (calls to customer service)?

**Insight:**  
- Users are generating a substantial volume of queries per month (with averages ranging from 90 to 160), while the number of customer service calls tends to be relatively low.  
- **Growth Opportunity:**  
  - Investigate if the low customer service calls indicate high product satisfaction or potential underreporting of issues.
  - Leverage analytics to identify heavy users and consider offering them premium support or feedback programs.
  - Develop self-service support resources to further reduce support overhead and improve user satisfaction.

---

## Question 3: What is the geographic distribution of our users and how can we tap into underrepresented regions?

**Insight:**  
- The majority of users are based in the USA, with a significant concentration in major tech hubs. There are also users from Europe (e.g., Ireland, UK, Germany) and China, though these regions are underrepresented compared to America.  
- **Growth Opportunity:**  
  - Expand international marketing efforts, particularly in Europe and Asia.
  - Localize platform features and support (e.g., multi-language support, region-specific case studies).
  - Explore partnerships with local technology communities and influencers in underrepresented regions to drive adoption.
